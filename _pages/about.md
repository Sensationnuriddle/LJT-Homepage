## About

Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group and graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. Research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with research interests in LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM), and LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability.

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2024-Present)
  - First-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group.
  - Supervisor: Professor Junxian He.
- **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (2020-2024)
  - Graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024.
  - Previously advised by Professor Junxian He during undergraduate studies at SJTU.
